Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Clipper, operated by GitHub user saad-naveed-brainx, uses and why. Clipper runs on your own computer, and most of your data never leaves it.

Information Clipper uses

  • Videos and clips: the videos you choose to process and the clips Clipper makes. These are stored on your computer.
  • Connected accounts: when you connect TikTok or YouTube, Clipper obtains an access token and basic profile information on your computer (for TikTok: your account's open ID, display name and avatar; for YouTube: your channel name and ID). They are stored on your computer, are not sent to us, and are used only to post clips you approve and to show which account is connected.
  • Posting settings: the titles, captions, privacy choices and schedule times you set for each clip.

How it is used

  • To find moments in a video, Clipper sends the video's YouTube link, or the video, to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Google processes this under the Gemini API Terms.
  • To post a clip, Clipper uploads it from your computer to TikTok or YouTube with the settings you chose.
  • We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services named above as needed to do what you ask.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your videos, clips and tokens stay on your computer until you delete them.
  • Disconnecting an account in Clipper deletes its token from your computer and asks the platform to revoke it. You can also remove Clipper's access from TikTok or YouTube settings at any time.
  • Clips you have posted stay on the platform until you delete them there.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move personal information about you, object to how it is used, and complain to your local data protection authority. Because Clipper keeps your data on your own computer, you can delete most of it yourself by removing your clips and disconnecting your accounts. For anything else, contact us below. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Security

Tokens are stored in a file only your user account can read. Connections to TikTok, YouTube and Google use encrypted HTTPS.

Children

Clipper is not intended for children under 13, or the minimum age required by TikTok or YouTube in your country.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
