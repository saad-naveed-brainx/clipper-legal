Clipper

Short clips from long gaming videos.

Clipper is a desktop tool that finds the best moments in long gaming videos, cuts them into vertical and landscape clips with captions and a title, and lets you post the clips you approve to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts at times you choose.

How posting works

  • You connect your own YouTube or TikTok account and can disconnect it at any time.
  • Nothing is posted until you approve the clip, its title and its settings.
  • Clips are uploaded from your computer straight to the platform you choose.
